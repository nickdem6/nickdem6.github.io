Dawnbell

A 2-bit action-adventure for iPhone

The Dawnbell is cracked and the night will not end. Win songs for your lyre, brave six dungeons and Dawnbell Keep, and ring in the dawn.

Questions

Where is my saved journey?
On your iPhone, in the game's own storage. It never leaves your phone, and deleting the app deletes it.
Do I need an internet connection?
No. Dawnbell plays entirely offline.
Can I play with a controller?
Yes. Touch controls are built in, and a game controller or a keyboard works too.
Does it work on a folding iPhone?
Yes. The controls lay themselves out again when you fold or unfold it.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, and no data collected.
