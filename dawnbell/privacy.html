Dawnbell Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Dawnbell does not collect, store or share any information about you.

What the game keeps

Your saved journey and your settings (such as whether sound is on) are kept only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them. If your device is backed up (to iCloud or to a computer), the backup may include them, as it includes other apps' data, under Apple's terms, not ours.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind, by us or by anyone else.
  • No advertising.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No internet connection: the game plays entirely offline.
  • It never asks for your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Children

Because the game collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If a future version of Dawnbell ever handles any information, this page will say so, with a new effective date, before that version is released.

Contact
